Where Do I See Myself in 4 Years?

In four years, I see myself working as a software engineer on a team that builds products people rely on every day, most likely in full-stack or backend development where I can design systems end to end. I want to get deeper into cloud-native architecture and scalable system design, and to be someone teammates trust with the harder design decisions, not just the code itself. I'm also seriously considering a master's degree in Software Engineering to sharpen my grounding in software architecture and large-scale system design. Alongside that, I'd like to keep contributing to open-source projects, since that's where I've learned the most outside the classroom. Whatever the exact title, I want to be the engineer who can take a vague problem and turn it into a well-structured, maintainable solution.
